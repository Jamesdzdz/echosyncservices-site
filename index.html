Mission

Consumer markets lie by omission. We fill in the blanks.

Prices shown per package instead of per useful unit, "promotions" computed on inflated reference prices, shipping that changes the whole equation, reviews nobody has time to read. Shoppers overpay, and brands fly blind. We fix both sides with the same data.

The real price

We normalize every offer to the unit that actually matters (a gram of protein, a serving, a dose), net of promotions and shipping. One number, comparable across any brand, pack size or merchant.

Independence as a rule

Rankings are never influenced by affiliation or advertising, and we never invent a data point. Unknown means not displayed. That is the moat a brand-funded comparator cannot copy.

Taste, from real buyers

We collect customer reviews from every merchant, in every language, and turn what buyers say about taste into a simple rank within each category. Our own calculation: no star copied from a merchant, no review republished, and nothing shown below a minimum number of reviews.

How we build

An AI-native company, from the first commit.

Echosync Services is a one-founder company that ships like a team. The whole WheyRadar stack, the website, the collectors for every merchant, the aggregation and quality gates, the deployment pipeline, was built with Claude Code, working from a written vision, a roadmap and a status file the way a small engineering team would.

In production, the Claude API drafts our weekly editorial pieces, and every figure and study is checked at the source by a human before anything is published. Next, it will read customer reviews at scale, topic by topic (taste, texture, mixability, value), for shoppers and for the brands behind the products.

Everything else is boring on purpose: static pages on the edge, one daily data cycle, a quality gate that blocks a deploy if the data looks degraded, and nothing invented, ever.
